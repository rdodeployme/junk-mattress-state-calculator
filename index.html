Before this goes to Richard — confirm these per state. The defaults are assumptions, not verified figures.
  • Landfill levy / residual disposal — differs materially between states and is a direct per-mattress cost.
  • Industrial rent and outgoings — get a real quote from a local agent for the precinct you'd actually take.
  • EPA / planning approval — some states require a licensed waste facility. Cost is manageable; the timeline is the risk.
  • Offtake for foam, textiles and timber — no local processor means that revenue is zero and freight goes up.
  • Contract volume — the model turns on the B2B percentage. Test at 0% before committing to anything.
  • Ramp — this shows a steady month. It does not show the 6–12 months of under-utilised fixed cost getting there.
